Reference
Doc 8168 Vol II II-4-1 (speeds Table II-4-1-2, 25° bank, ISA+15, timing §1.3.2.2, buffer MOC Table II-4-1-1) and I-4-3-App C §3.3 (template Table I-4-3-App C-4). The drawn basic area is the template swept around the fix tolerance area (convex envelope of the wind circles). The omnidirectional entry area follows §3.3.3.2.1 (overhead a VOR/NDB): curve 5 = template with point E on the circle through A1/A3; curves 6–7 = template on the 70° sector 1/3 line at the entry fix area, curve 8 their mirror; arc about A to curve 1. Only the template ahead of "a" is used for curves 6–8, and the outline is the convex envelope — an approximation of the hand construction. The 5 NM buffer is drawn around basic + entry area (II-4-1 §1.3.12.1). For radial-intersection or RNAV fixes use the entry construction for that fix type instead. VOR/DME (§3.3.4): Ds, DL (Doc rounding), DLs and d1/d2 are exact; the basic area is the convex envelope of the template at A1–A4 and turned 180° at the C-points on DL1/DL2, with the outbound limits drawn parallel to the inbound track and without the limiting-radial reduction (§3.3.4.1.1.4 e) — conservative. Checked against the Doc worked examples: DL exact (35 / 26 NM); with entries the area is within about 0.8 NM of Diagram C-13 (towards) but up to about 2 NM smaller than Diagram C-15 (away) — finish the design with the Doc construction. Entry areas: sector 1 along the DME arc (curves 13, 15) and sector 2 for 1 / 1.5 min (curve 11); the reciprocal direct entry to a secondary point (§3.3.4.1.3) is not drawn. Radial intersection (§3.3.3.2.2): entry along the reciprocal of the inbound track (curves 5·6) and, when the intersecting VOR bearing is given and lies in sector 2 or the part of sector 3 opposite it, along the intersecting radial (curves 7, 8, both turn directions). Not built: the area reduction when sector 1 entries are not permitted (§3.3.3.3) — the text and Diagram C-11 do not fix the position of the parallel line unambiguously.
